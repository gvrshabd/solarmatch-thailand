# Installer launch decisions and activation

This is a prelaunch local implementation. Code completion means the guarded path exists; it does not mean an account, legal term, delivery route, bank settlement or deployment has been approved. The provider gates and protected local test steps are in [provider setup](INSTALLER_PROVIDER_SETUP.md) and [local testing](installer-local-test.md). Current repair passed 256 unit tests, typecheck, lint and both builds. Broad browsers had 156 passes, 16 intentional skips and one Safari navigation timeout; the identical case passed alone on rerun. Separate installer/admin acceptance passed six/four cases. The final sixteen-migration synthetic restore passed 55 tables and 23 triggers. No live operation is authorised by these results; collection, payments, outbound messaging and automatic allocation remain disabled.

## 1. Fixed and locally verified

### Autonomous preparation after the repair baseline

Campaign capture/report/export and structured campaign/day advertising spend are now coded with exact catalogue validation, original acquisition snapshots, environment separation and privacy redaction. Both activation/privacy flags remain off. General analytics `track.ts` remains inert. Job ownership/health, local deduplicated alerts and secret-safe preflight are implemented; cron and external alert routing remain absent. SELECT-only shutdown liability review and CSV distinguish calculation, approval and money returned, flagging unresolved funding/price evidence rather than guessing. Local object recovery and extended D1 consent/cap checks are prepared. The new chain has eighteen migrations; only isolated synthetic data was migrated.

Confirmed owner decisions: father's private limited company, current /installers architecture, merchant/domain registration deferred, seven-day invalid-enquiry claim submission (credit restoration, not automatic cash refund), and refund unused purchased credits on permanent shutdown. Only exact company/tax/legal facts, detailed evidence/eligibility/expiry/refund implementation remain unresolved; the shutdown-refund principle is no longer undecided. See `installer-claim-shutdown-review.txt`.

LINE account UI inspection timed out twice; no actual OA identity, channel or recipient was verified. No approved HTTPS sandbox endpoint, verified Resend sender/domain or email destination is available. Thai Twilio custom-body acceptance/free allowance is not verified; current new-console trial templates and legacy test credentials are different capabilities. No SMS/LINE/email was sent. See current official sources and domain quotes in `installer-provider-domain-research.txt`.

All nine reviewed defects have repairs and retained synthetic regressions: referral hours including closing batches and retry accounting; withdrawn owner-scoped financial records and original claim access; linked canonical/duplicate purge notes and audits; implemented secure payment-return status; trusted production/sandbox/local-test/restricted-preview classification; final-submission pause through the commit boundary; purchased-credit restoration against debt; selected approved payment methods; and authenticated company-owner LINE unlink with pending-link invalidation, retained notification status and later reconnection. See the per-defect reproduction, final results and honest timeout/retry history in [implementation status](INSTALLER_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md). The full 20-case mobile WebKit audit passed on sequential rerun. The observed local IPv4 preview workaround is not a proven production navigation repair; preserve its documented diagnostic limitation.

Further reproduced route/boundary gaps were repaired within the same scope: withdrawn deliveries still allowed outcome writes; free-text claim reasons/decisions could expose homeowner details after withdrawal; test requests sharing a phone/property could contaminate production canonical identity; a previously queued local/sandbox referral could be sent by a production outbox worker; withdrawal during settings or a later clock read could leave a stale homeowner SMS recipient; and unlink during LINE token acquisition could create a fresh stale challenge. Active-access outcome writes, redacted financial-only claims, mode-specific canonical identity, pre-lease and final provider-context/consent checks with a synchronous final time check, and monotonic LINE-link generation now retain regression coverage. Mismatched notification contexts remain visible as action-required without consuming delivery attempts. In-flight provider acceptance remains evidence for review, not proof that cancellation recalled a message.

The current ordered schema has eighteen migrations. Earlier repair migrations `0014`–`0016` protect debt-first credits, final submission/allocation classification and LINE-link generation. New `0017` adds privacy-gated campaign attribution/spend; `0018` adds scheduler leases and local alerts. They are additive and local only; historical migration labels, recipient slots and consent facts are preserved. No remote database was read or migrated.

## 2. Awaiting provider and production acceptance

Retain the existing launch tasks: provider credentials and sender/domain ownership; Omise merchant methods, authorised return/reconciliation/reversal and bank settlement; controlled Thai SMS handset delivery and provider delivery evidence; LINE Official Account linking/unlinking, signed webhooks, retry behavior and device testing; production authentication/Cloudflare metadata acceptance; scheduled processing/alert ownership; and SolarMatch-only backup, object inventory, restore and migration rehearsal on a protected disposable copy. Mocked transports and compiled local pages do not establish deliverability, settlement or production acceptance.

A separate installer subdomain needs an explicit origin/authentication design and tests. Current request validation is tied to `INSTALLER_BASE_URL`; DNS/routing alone cannot establish accepted homeowner origins, cookie scope, CSRF/CORS behavior or authentication/payment/LINE callbacks. No domain configuration changes were made. Production scheduling, deployment, remote migrations and every live provider switch remain separately authorised launch steps.

## 3. Awaiting owner and legal policy decisions

Confirm company/operator legal identity, contact details, address and registration; VAT/tax treatment and invoice requirements; retention, legal holds and backup deletion; credit expiry and the detailed shutdown-refund approval/execution process; claim evidence/eligibility and investigation process; qualification and authorised property-owner criteria; freshness/duplicate windows; and staff-sharing expiry/notices. The principle of refunding unused purchased credits on shutdown is confirmed, not awaiting a new owner decision. Publish reviewed Thai/English terms and renew company acceptance for material changes. The current test defaults are not legal acceptance. No retention period, refund deadline or automatic deletion was invented.

## 4. Deliberately deferred features

Campaign attribution is implemented but privacy/activation gated off; a third-party analytics provider and general anonymous event tracking remain deliberately absent. `lib/analytics/track.ts` is still a no-op. Owner/legal review of exact catalogue and functional tab-session campaign storage is required before activation.

Exclusive/factory/commercial products, automatic provider refund execution, certified tax invoicing, buyer auctions, public coupons, advertising automation, large-history pagination and team invitation administration remain deferred. No cron, DNS, merchant method or production configuration was enabled as part of this repair.
