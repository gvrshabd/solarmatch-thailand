# Legal research inputs

Checked: **2026-09-01**

These official sources are research inputs only. They do not prove that SolarMatch is compliant and do not replace advice from qualified Thai counsel.

- Thailand PDPC/GPPC Privacy Policy: https://gppc.pdpc.or.th/privacy-policy/
  - Useful structural reference for controller contact details, processing purposes/bases, external recipients, international transfers, retention, security, data-subject rights, complaints, and change notices.
- Thailand PDPC/GPPC Cookie Policy: https://gppc.pdpc.or.th/cookie-policy/
  - Useful structural reference for explaining cookie purpose, categories, duration, external parties, and browser controls. SolarMatch's policy must describe SolarMatch's actual technologies rather than copying GPPC's cookie inventory.
- ETDA electronic-transactions law collection: https://www.etda.or.th/th/Useful-Resource/law/transactionlaws.aspx
  - Contains the current official electronic-transactions legislation and guidance links. Classification and contracting conclusions remain counsel-review items.
- Thailand DBD online services: https://www.dbd.go.th/en/hotService/online_service
- DBD Registered verification service: https://dbdregistered.dbd.go.th/th/searchLogo
  - Operator registration and display facts must be verified against the actual entity record before publication.
- OCPB direct-marketing reference supplied for review: https://www.ocpb.go.th/images/article/article_20260427104524.pdf
  - Direct-marketing and consumer-protection classification remains unresolved pending qualified review.

The implementation deliberately keeps contact collection disabled while operator facts, legal review, retention/distribution periods, and partner-contract facts are incomplete.
