SolarMatch trust content — unpublished working draft
Reviewed internally: 2026-10-19. Do not display on public pages until owner and partner evidence below is supplied and checked in both languages.

1. How participating installers are selected
Safe current framing: SolarMatch is preparing a referral service. A homeowner may view the free planning estimate without requesting contact. If contact sharing is activated, eligibility and delivery limits are separate from the calculator result; a request may reach fewer than three companies or none. Participation does not mean government accreditation or a guarantee of workmanship, price, or suitability.
Before publication, owner must document the actual company onboarding checks, who performs each check, evidence inspected, expiry/recheck process, service-area and capacity rules, and complaint/removal policy. State only checks that have been operating and evidenced. Do not use "vetted", "certified", "top-ranked" or a verification badge until its exact test and records exist.

2. How SolarMatch earns money
Safe current framing: The calculator is free to homeowners. The planned referral/installer-credit model may charge participating installers for delivered enquiries; trial deliveries may also occur. A referral fee does not make SolarMatch the installer or bind a homeowner to buy. The live public contact and credit purchases remain disabled at this review date.
Before publication, owner must confirm the effective commercial terms, whether trial/paid treatment affects allocation, any other revenue sources, whether a payment is made only after documented delivery, and the approved plain-language disclosure. Confirm the legal operator details and privacy contact; the current legal copy contains placeholders.

3. Factual installer profile template (no sample profile)
Fields to evidence before publication: legal company name and registration number; trading name; verified business address and service areas; named contact role (with consent); installation licences/qualifications relevant to the actual work; insurer and coverage dates if claimed; supported system types and phase; actual quote and aftercare responsibilities; equipment/warranty providers and claim route; profile reviewed date. Keep unknown fields unpublished or mark them "Not provided" only in an agreed public profile format.
Before publication, collect partner consent to publish, documentary evidence for each claim, expiry/recheck dates, and an owner-approved correction process. No partner names, reviews, rankings, performance statistics, warranties or technical-inspection claims are supported by this draft.
