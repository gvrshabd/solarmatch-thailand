## Implemented workflow

Verified email company accounts use maintained Better Auth with persistent D1 sessions, salted password hashing, recovery and logout. Company memberships are separate from users. The Thai-first mobile portal provides profile, service areas, capacity, pause, terms, credit history, payments, inbox, claim submission and LINE linking. Private APIs enforce membership and verified email; account-free staff links expose only their assigned delivery and no financial/account functions. Links accept a bearer token from the fragment and remove it immediately from browser history. Tokens are hashed server-side, expire, rotate and revoke independently of financial delivery records.

The additive migrations keep legacy enquiries unverified and in review. The credit ledger records integer deltas and rejects overdrafts and edits. Database delivery triggers enforce consent, eligibility, company approval, areas, terms, capacity, funding, delivery hours and lifetime recipient limits, while committing the charge, rotation and durable outbox together. Trial units are separate from purchased credits and restricted to shared deliveries. Omise hosted components, server confirmation and bounded charge reconciliation support sandbox and deliberately configured live modes; all live switches and purchases remain off. Provider credentials and merchant activation remain external requirements.

## Commercial and legal launch configuration

The current draft model is ฿200 per credit, two credits for a shared residential enquiry, 20 credits for ฿4,000, two free shared enquiries per approved company, and a seven-day claim submission deadline. Delivery means availability in the receiving company's portal, not a clipboard copy, accepted notification or proven reading. Investigation may continue after the submission deadline; credit restoration is distinct from cash refund and never reopens a recipient slot. Exclusive residential support remains disabled; factory/commercial offers are deferred.

Required unresolved settings include operator legal identity, contact/address/registration details, VAT status and tax wording, invoice requirements, approved merchant and bank settlement accounts, activated payment methods, credit expiry and unused-credit/refund policy, final claim eligibility, enquiry freshness, qualification assumptions, share-link expiry and retention periods. Development defaults are not legal approval. Historical contracts and versioned consent snapshots remain historical evidence. Current public legal copy must be reviewed against the new draft before activation; archived legal-review drafts containing obsolete pricing or deadlines must not be presented as launch terms.

Current configurable test defaults are owner required, at least four installed AC units and quality score at least 4/5, with separate verified-phone and referral-consent checks. The original calculator/scoring model is unchanged. Distribution is seven days, duplicate review is thirty days, staff links expire after seven days, and retention is unset. These are visible configuration defaults, not finalized legal policies. Every new submission records the evaluated criteria and outcomes for administrator explanation. Changing material commercial terms requires a new version and renewed company acceptance; earlier acceptance timestamps are preserved.

## Activation checklist

A separate installer subdomain requires an explicit origin/authentication design and tests. Current request validation ties requests to `INSTALLER_BASE_URL`; changing DNS or routing alone does not establish trusted origins, session-cookie behavior, CORS/CSRF protection, public homeowner submission origins or authentication/payment/LINE callback behavior. Domain configuration was not changed.

6. Review company approval/terms, service areas, qualification settings, trial grants, financial reconciliation, withdrawal, share access and manual/automatic allocation checks.
7. Configure operational alerts and scheduled queue/outbox processing in a separately approved deployment; no cron is enabled in this implementation.
8. Activate collection, payments, allocation and outbound messaging separately after documented acceptance. Domain/subdomain changes require separate authorization.

## Privacy redaction and retention

The installed Better Auth 1.7.7 IP parser references `zod.validate`, which is absent from installed Zod 4.4.3. The compatibility workaround was reviewed and retained rather than changing authentication dependencies during this release. The application handles authentication IP throttling through its own atomic D1 HTTP gate using trusted Cloudflare request metadata, strips untrusted IP headers, and disables the library's IP tracking/internal limiter. Limits remain 30 requests per minute overall, five password sign-ins per minute, three registrations per minute, and three reset/verification requests per five minutes. Earlier local Workerd signup verified the path; a supported dependency correction requires separate compatibility and rate-limit regression checks before removal.

Once the installer schema is present, the existing authenticated, CSRF-protected admin purge action redacts canonical homeowner records and associated notes, score explanations, export snapshots, claim notes and audit payloads even when installer collection is switched off. It preserves non-personal canonical tombstones, financial records and immutable lifetime recipient slots. Legal holds abort the complete batch. Canonical withdrawal immediately suppresses further allocation, revokes staff tokens, cancels pending notifications and creates privacy tasks for companies already supplied. These tasks require responsible-person action; copies already downloaded cannot be retracted. Redaction is irreversible without restoring an authorized backup.

The retention helper previews candidates only after an explicit duration is supplied; scheduled jobs do not apply an invented legal retention period. Existing R2 media assets have no homeowner lead relationship, and partner contract documents belong to companies; this workflow does not delete those unrelated objects. Administrator ledger reasons should contain operational explanations only, never homeowner contact details, because the financial ledger remains immutable.
