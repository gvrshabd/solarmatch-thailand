SolarMatch claim review and shutdown accounting — internal review only
11 October 2026

Confirmed owner decisions

The operating company will be the owner's father's private limited company.
Exact legal identity, registration, tax details and lawyer-approved wording remain
unavailable. The current architecture remains /installers; domain and merchant
registration are deferred.

The invalid-enquiry claim SUBMISSION window is seven days from recorded original
availability in the receiving company's portal. The owner reviews evidence and
determines eligibility. An approved claim restores credits. This is not an
automatic seven-day cash-refund guarantee. Investigation can continue after a
timely submission. Restoration never reopens the lifetime three-company slot.

Unused purchased credits should be refunded if SolarMatch shuts down. No shutdown
refund deadline, credit expiry, payment channel or new eligibility term has been
decided by this preparation. No refund is approved or executed by the report.

Seven-day claim review using the existing protected admin flow

1. Confirm the company, delivery reference, funding source, original available_at,
   recorded claim_deadline and submitted_at. Current claimDays must agree with the
   confirmed seven-day submission policy before launch. Preserve original facts;
   do not reset deadlines on notification retries, viewing or withdrawal.
2. Verify company-scoped submission and timely receipt. Withdrawal retains the
   redacted financial record and claim route; it does not restore homeowner data
   access or staff links. Read evidence only through the authorised admin review.
3. Review the supplied reason and evidence. No answer or no resulting sale alone
   does not establish invalidity. Detailed eligibility/evidence rules still need
   owner and legal review; do not invent automatic rejection/approval rules.
4. Use the existing decide-claim action: needs-information, approved or rejected,
   with the required administrative rationale. Requesting evidence does not create
   a new seven-day cash-refund period. Avoid unnecessary homeowner personal data
   in administrative notes; immutable financial ledger reasons must be nonpersonal.
5. Verify approved purchased restoration repays any existing purchased-credit debt
   first, with only the remainder becoming available. Trial restoration remains a
   trial entitlement. Confirm one-time ledger issuance and unchanged recipient
   slots. Repeat approval must not create another restoration.
6. Communicate the reasoned decision using the reviewed Thai/English wording after
   separate approval. No customer or provider message was sent during preparation.

Shutdown accounting and manual refund preparation

1. Use separately authorised shutdown controls to stop collection, purchases,
   allocation and referral messaging. Preserve calculator results, claim access,
   necessary record access and the original claim submission deadlines. This
   document does not execute those controls or promise ongoing service duration.
2. Preserve a protected financial snapshot and export, respecting legal holds and
   retention requirements. Resolve outstanding claims, reservations, ambiguous
   charge attempts and provider reconciliation errors through the protected admin
   flow. Do not create another payment to resolve an uncertain existing charge.
3. Generate getShutdownLiabilityReport and shutdownLiabilityCsv from the financial
   snapshot. They perform SELECT-only reads of accounts, immutable ledger, orders,
   claims and delivery funding facts. No provider call, ledger change, approval or
   refund execution occurs. They include archived companies' financial accounts.
4. Review available/reserved credits, trial entitlements, promotional/unpriced
   adjustments, test/fixture sources, reversal debt and provider-recorded prior
   refunds separately. Synthetic calculations are excluded from live-money totals.
   Current settings' nominal credit price is not historical payment evidence.
5. The report calculates historical paid value only where evidence establishes the
   relevant unused purchased units and price. If every remaining evidenced purchase
   is unused, it can sum retained paid amounts even at different historical prices.
   With partial consumption and different historical prices, the existing fungible
   ledger has no debit-to-purchase-lot allocation: no FIFO or average-cost policy is
   assumed. Mixed promotional/manual/test funding, fractional minor units and
   nonproportional partial refunds are flagged for review rather than assigned an
   invented exact refund. Review-required companies make totals incomplete.
6. Reconcile each original order and recorded refund with later authorised provider
   evidence and settlement/bank records. A stored provider refund amount is not proof
   that a bank has returned money. Do not double-refund previously processed sums.
   Debt is displayed for review, not automatically netted against a cash refund.
7. Maintain a separately approved refund register: company/order references,
   calculation and supporting snapshot, owner approval/amount/date, execution
   reference/status and evidence that money was returned. These are distinct states.
   This code introduces no approval register or refund executor; an authoritative
   tracking workflow and payment method need approval before actual refunds.
8. After separately authorised manual execution, reconcile provider evidence using
   the existing guarded service, confirm ledger conservation and retain necessary
   audit evidence. Repeat handling must not pay or restore credit twice. Customer
   notices, payment instructions and any deadline remain future approved work.

Draft English wording — REVIEW ONLY, not published launch terms

Submit an invalid-enquiry claim within seven days of the enquiry first becoming
available in your company's SolarMatch portal. We review the evidence and decide
whether the claim is eligible. Approved claims restore the applicable credits;
review may finish after the submission window. Credit restoration is different
from a cash refund and does not reopen a recipient slot. Withdrawal retains claim
access to a redacted financial record and revokes access to homeowner information.

If SolarMatch closes permanently, unused purchased credits will be refunded.
The refund calculation, approval and evidence that money has been returned are
separate steps. The detailed process and final reviewed terms will be confirmed
before commercial launch. This draft sets no refund deadline or credit expiry.
