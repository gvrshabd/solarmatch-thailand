Necessary departures from obsolete prototype data and copy
- Actual energy-v3 model drives every displayed figure and the lifetime plot.
  Matched synthetic THB6000/Sathon/high-use/single-phase fixture has 5.005kWp,
  THB170130, approximately THB2755 monthly reduction, 5.4-year simple payback,
  THB574776.42 lifetime benefit; untouched prototype shows its legacy figures.
  No fixture output, old engine, fake extras math or obsolete numeric expectation
  is inserted into the application to make screenshots match.
- “Reference bill” labels the modern before/after, and percentage uses a reference
  bill. This is necessary because additional demand/current tariffs can make the
  model baseline differ from the entered historical bill.
- A visible reference-scenario note next to the lifetime plot reports actual
  immediate self-use percentage (~97.7% for this fixture) and explicitly says
  smooth assumed load/solar curves are uncalibrated, not measured performance.
  Attribution is documented separately in docs/calculator-v3/BENEFIT_ATTRIBUTION.txt.
- Real output precision needs 18px statistics below400px (and narrower horizontal
  stat padding below375px); labels remain readable at13px. The prototype's shorter
  4.5kWp/165000 values do not expose the same width constraint.
  Exact quoted cash prices retain cents. A long-price-only adaptation reduces
  its numeral size/padding so the full amount stays inside its own statistic,
  including320/360/390/414px mobile and1440px desktop; no value rounding is used
  to hide this layout constraint. Final browser verification is recorded below.
- Extras retain substantive progressive-tax eligibility and bracket calculations,
  actual-quote finance and interval/contract export controls. Three native closed
  disclosures use the approved row/switch appearance. Expanded real controls are
  necessarily larger than the obsolete single-tax-rate prototype. No ownership
  gate, automatic tax credit, automatic export price/term or invented payback.
- Link-content privacy disclosure, conditional states and full diagnostics/legal
  content add height absent from the mock. Negative net, increased/unchanged
  bills, unavailable payback, unsupported/no-feasible/no-economic results keep
  honest guards and diagnostic access. Header/footer are shared-shell ownership.
  Existing44px minimum touch targets are retained versus40px prototype buttons.
