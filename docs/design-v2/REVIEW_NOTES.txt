Simple payback and long-term cumulative-cost crossing are not the same measure. A 24.8-year simple payback can coexist with a negative 25-year cumulative net once maintenance and degradation are counted. The arithmetic should not be changed merely to remove the apparent contradiction.

Recommended owner decision: a clearer negative financial verdict under the stated assumptions. Pending that decision, retain the current recommendation logic plus Claude's explicit warning. The revised executor prompt does not assume approval of a new verdict.

COPY THAT NEEDS FACTUAL CHECKING
Exact visual design does not require repeating misleading product promises.
- "You choose who contacts you": current workflow lets a homeowner request contact and matches up to three approved installers; it does not necessarily offer a company-selection UI.
- "No sales calls": needs to be qualified to the estimate/no-request journey.
- Instant LINE wording: notifications follow the agreed Bangkok business-hours policy, account linkage and actual provider availability.
- "We email you": must match the actual approval notification implementation/readiness.
- Competitor comparisons and policy/tax/export claims need substantiation and current eligibility wording; this design review did not independently re-research Thai regulations.
- The pack lists photo photographers, but no original photo-page URLs/license capture. Preserve the assets and obtain provenance before publication if not already held.

DESIGN COVERAGE LIMITS
The core screens have executable visual references. Many secondary states do not: true authentication/recovery, enabled consent/OTP, detailed claims, actual terms, payment returns, staff-link access/expiry, withdrawal, account dropdown, and content/legal pages.
The default implementation plan preserves those working screens and applies Claude's shared component styling. A claim of exact reproduction should be limited to screens with an actual reference.
If the owner wants Claude-designed compositions for those missing screens as well, request a targeted addendum; do not commission a new redesign of the already supplied core screens.
