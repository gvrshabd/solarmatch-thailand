SolarMatch calculator accuracy v3 verification
Scope: local synthetic inputs only; no remote database/provider operations.

Automated evidence
- tests/unit/calculator-accuracy-v3.test.ts integrates calculateEstimate with
  explicit utility, bill/reference dates, tariff category, usable roof and phase.
  Physical checks: 1 m² rejects every real package; a 20 kWp quote cannot fit a
  20 m² roof; unknown phase remains conditional; finite candidate selection
  maximizes positive 25-year net value over no investment; low demand selects
  no investment. Exact cash quotation cents reach costs, reserve and cash flow.
  Energy checks: monthly/annual demand and generation conservation; bill and
  meter kWh/day normalization; existing appliance schedules reallocate the
  original bill demand; quantified future EV, AC and pump use their own units
  and first active month/year. Appliance names alone add no guessed demand.
  Tariff checks: August historical inversion is separate from October future
  bill valuation; unsupported TOU/category/date withholds recommendation.
  Financial check: independently rebuild year-25 energy and progressive bills,
  demonstrating that degraded money is not a scaled year-one savings value.
  Limits: horizontal azimuth invariance, coordinates and installed count alone
  do not create site-specific production or demand.
- tests/unit/energy-model-v3.test.ts verifies all 8760 hourly intervals,
  monthly/annual conservation, zero night generation, inverter clipping,
  calendar weekday/weekend shapes, existing-load capping, timed future-load
  increments, solar geometry, explicit synthetic resource limits and catalogue
  equipment/price/source consistency. Do not duplicate hourly loops in the
  integration suite.
- tests/unit/tariff-accuracy-v3.test.ts checks every tier boundary and reverse
  calculation, category distinctions, date boundaries, missing-date warnings,
  source/effective/checked/expiry metadata and absence of indefinite fallback.

collection release. The current leads table requires a positive actual bill,
and published scoring uses actual-bill bands. /api/leads returns 422
contact_consumption_input_not_supported before token/proof/database work for
every kWh contact payload, including one accompanied by a positive bill; an
old questionnaire token cannot authorize a new consumption submission mode.
The free no-contact meter-only estimate remains available. No user bill is
fabricated and owner plus four installed AC qualification is unchanged.
Real meter-only contact collection requires an explicit versioned storage and
published scoring integration plus normal questionnaire/release publication;
neither activation nor a migration is part of this work.
When consumptionInput is explicitly kwh with positive monthlyConsumptionKwh,
an absent monthlyBillThb becomes zero solely as the legacy numeric-field
compatibility sentinel. It is not a supplied or inferred bill; meter kWh is
authoritative. Bill mode still requires a positive supplied amount. The
existing qualification bill factor consequently remains zero/conservative for
meter-only inputs; staging collection is disabled. Never label this sentinel
as the homeowner's actual bill or use it to infer meter consumption.
