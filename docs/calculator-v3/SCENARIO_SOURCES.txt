PERSONAL SOLAR TAX RULE VERSION

1. Royal Gazette, Royal Decree 805 (B.E. 2569)
https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/104277.pdf
Published 2 March 2026; effective the following day. Personal qualifying
equipment/installation exemption is capped at THB 200,000, one system/one claim,
with the window ending 31 December 2028. This is an income exemption, not an
installation rebate. No entitlement is established by this calculator.
Verification limit: PDF extraction for this Gazette copy is font-encoded and
largely unreadable. Gazette metadata is readable; the operative implementation
details below were directly readable in the Revenue Department implementing PDF.

2. Revenue Department, Director-General income-tax notification 470
https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/dg470A.pdf
Clauses 2–6: qualifying individual, residential type-1 MEA/PEA supply, installed
capacity at most 10 kW, qualifying building, one system/claim; agreement,
application and purchase/installation proof within 3 March 2026–31 December 2028.
Signed 30 September 2026; claim in successful connection year. Same claimant must match account,
application, approval and invoice taxpayer ID. Eligible supplier e-Tax invoice,
connection documents and utility notification are required; reuse of expenditure
for other relief or input-VAT deduction is excluded. Special spouse filing rules
exist. The additional invoice solar-description requirement applies to invoices
issued from 30 September 2026, the signing date (clause 8).
Verification limits: supplier eligibility, full invoice
compliance, timing of utility notification, document identity and tax filing
facts are caller-confirmed checklist facts, not verified by the app. Source
says total installed kW without an explicit DC/AC basis; the calculator does not
silently relabel it kWp or the export AC limit. Complex joint/spouse allocations
are outside this single-claimant model. Prospective facts are intentions only.

3. Revenue Department, ordinary personal-income-tax brackets
https://www.rd.go.th/59670.html
Title references tax year 2560, with application from that year onward; page
last updated 2021-01-09. Net-income bands: first THB150,000 exempt; next to
300,000 at 5%; to 500,000 at 10%; to 750,000 at 15%; to 1m at 20%; to 2m at
25%; to 5m at 30%; remainder 35%. The implementation taxes the ordinary net
income before and after min(qualifying spend, 200,000, net income), rather than
multiplying all expenditure by the top marginal rate. Example: 800,000 gives
75,000 tax; 600,000 gives 42,500; saving is 32,500, not 40,000 or 200,000.
Limit: no claim that this old page was revised in 2026, nor that this arithmetic
models minimum-tax alternatives, tax credits, filing rounding, other allowances,
or final RD assessment. Input is net taxable income before solar, not gross pay.
The optional calculation requires explicit ordinary-progressive tax confirmation;
unknown, alternative-tax, credit and complex-filing cases show no precise amounts.
This calculation-basis choice is memory-only, separate from legal eligibility.

EXPORT MODEL LIMITS (implementation decisions, not programme entitlements)
Base export income remains zero. Optional revenue needs explicit verified rate,
start, inclusive end and AC export limit from the user's contract, plus interval
surplus after home consumption. Contract calendar uses Thailand UTC+07:00.
Each interval exports min(surplus, AC limit * interval hours); time outside
contract is excluded. Partial boundary intervals use an expressly disclosed
constant interval-average approximation. Overlapping/invalid intervals are
rejected. This is not a reconstruction of instantaneous peaks from annual
energy. No annual extrapolation, 20-year extension or automatic programme rate
is inserted. No intervals means insufficient information, not a made-up income.

FINANCE MODEL LIMITS (implementation arithmetic, not lender information)
No bank, rate or product is invented. User supplies actual deposit, term,
annual rate and flat/reducing basis. Principal = cost - deposit + financed fees.
Flat interest = principal * annual rate * years. Reducing balance uses a fixed
monthly rate of annual rate / 12 and an equal-payment annuity. Zero rate is
handled separately. Upfront fees affect initial cash; financed fees affect
principal. Omitted fees are assumed zero ONLY for arithmetic and flagged unknown.
An optional actual quoted monthly payment overrides cash-flow payment, not
modelled interest. If it differs from calculated payment, final instalment,
fees and residual debt require checking. Scheduled total is payment * months;
it is not asserted to settle every quote's balloon or irregular schedule.
Financing is separate from electricity savings. UI net cash flow subtracts
repayment and average reserve; tax/export are not automatically included.

UI AND PRIVACY
OptionalScenarios inputs are local React state only, with no form submission,
storage, URL, logging, analytics, lead fields or API calls. Tax fields are absent
from EstimateAnswers. Caller must pass only the documented energy/cost props.
Clear button resets optional fields. Module functions have no side effects.
Tax benefit is one-time and conditional, shown separately from installation
invoice and monthly bill, with lifetime benefit both before and after relief.
