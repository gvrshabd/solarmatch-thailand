Energy and financial definitions
The representative calendar is 2027, with 8,760 one-hour intervals. Self-use is
min(solar,demand) in every interval; night demand remains grid demand. Existing
appliances reallocate bill consumption, never add it twice. Contradictory schedules
are scaled to the bill with a visible warning. Planned loads add quantified use
from their starting calendar month/year, not a generic percentage; partial-month
proration is not modeled and the UI says so.
JRC PVGIS-ERA5 monthly reference: 2005–2023, Bangkok 13.75N/100.5E, 15 degrees
south, building-integrated crystalline silicon, DEM horizon, 14% system loss.
Annual yield 1398.23 kWh/kWp; source monthly rounding totals1398.22. Shares are
normalized, no second generic loss is deducted. Full source JSON and a verified
read-only reproduction script are included. Hourly disaggregation, household
profiles and adjustments to other panel planes remain approximate/unvalidated.
Household coordinates do not falsely select a local weather dataset.
Package catalogue: four finite CX ENERTECH configurations, source27September2026,
checked17October2026. Panel DC = module count x Wp; inverter AC is separate.
Starting prices plus7% contingency are not confirmed VAT or binding quotations.
Physical fit, phase, budget and positive25-year value determine automatic choices.
No feasible/economic option is an explicit outcome; no fictitious tiny package.
Exact quotations retain cents. Unknown battery scope, contradictory specifications
and unsupported capacity do not receive financial recommendations.
Annual reserve1.02% includes component/inverter risk once. Generation degrades0.5%
and each year's energy matching and progressive bills are recalculated. Simple
payback, cumulative crossing, operating cash flow and discounted value differ.
Base results exclude export revenue, personal tax relief and finance.

Known model / evidence limits
- No measured household profile or site-specific weather validation. Smooth hourly
  representative days can overstate self-use when real generation/load fluctuates.
- Source mounting configuration is a reference, not proof of a household's mounting,
  shading, structural fit, clipping, string design or utility approval.
- One supplier's starting catalogue is not a competitive market average. Real site
  quotations and VAT/included-scope confirmation remain necessary.
- Independent MEA page verification failed; PEA tables and official Ft were checked.
- Bill date is a bill-month tariff reference, not meter-interval tariff proration.
  Welfare/free-electricity credits, utility classification changes, arrears and
  line-item rounding are not replicated. Tariff-category selection needs the bill.
- TOU, battery dispatch/backup and existing-system expansion are unsupported.
  Explicit solar-only illustrations must not be mistaken for those project models.
- Other provinces use a clearly conditional regional illustration, not local yield.
- Sensitivity cases are authored assumptions, not statistical confidence intervals.
- Tax status is conditional, not a refund/approval; complex tax cases are outside
  ordinary progressive PIT. Contract export uses supplied intervals only. Finance
  uses actual entered terms, not lender offers or an effective-APR calculation.

Contact readiness — intentional safety boundary, not completed activation
Collection, payments, notifications, attribution and automatic allocation remain
disabled/paused. No real message or charge is sent.
The historical lead-storage/scoring contract requires a positive actual bill.
Meter-only calculator input uses zero only as a legacy numeric compatibility
sentinel, never a fictitious bill. /api/leads explicitly rejects kWh contact mode
with safe422 before token/proof/database work, rather than returning a SQL500.
Live meter-input enquiries need a deliberate versioned storage/qualification
decision and compatible published release. Do not publish/activate v7 for real
collection on the assumption this work implements that separate integration.
Owner plus four installed AC hard gates are unchanged. Daylight operating counts
must not substitute for installed counts. Existing bill-based authenticated
submission remains on its original validation/security path.
