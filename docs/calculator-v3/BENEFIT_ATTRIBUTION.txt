LIFETIME BENEFIT ATTRIBUTION — synthetic daytime THB6000 fixture
Local investigation, 17 October 2026. No calculator model changes.

Result
The published THB263000 -> THB574776.42 comparison is reproducible. It does
not establish a defect or an accuracy improvement. Most of the increase is
the change from a 70% self-consumption assumption to the hourly model's very
high coincidence of smooth, repeated solar and demand curves. The source
documents explicitly call those hourly curves unvalidated assumptions.
Tax relief, export income, finance and tariff escalation contribute zero.

Diagnostic implementation: scripts/calculator-benefit-attribution-v3.ts.
It prints BENEFIT_ATTRIBUTION_JSON with full precision, both paths, every

It does not write files, invoke services, change prices or select a new system.

Counterfactual convention
Eight binary switches keep six requested categories distinct and retain two
additional material implementation changes explicitly:
  size: 4.5 -> 5.005 DC kWp, price held independently;
  price: upfront CAPEX 165000 -> 170130, reserve held independently;
  generation: legacy Bangkok 1380*.95 and inherited monthly shares -> JRC
    1398.23/reference panel plane and source monthly shares;
  selfConsumption: monthly min(demand, .70*PV) -> hourly min(demand, PV);
  tariff: frozen legacy schedule -> dated v3 schedule for these same dates;
  operatingCost: annual absolute reserve 1683 -> 1735.326;
  demandCalendar: twelve identical kWh months -> days-normalized months;
  degradationMethod: degrade year-one monetary savings -> degrade generation
    then recompute coincidence and bills each year, at the same 0.5%/year.

Price and reserve switches intentionally separate two effects of the real
price change; both must switch to recover v3. Annual reserve rate itself did
not change (1.02% CAPEX). Likewise annual demand did not change: both engines
infer 15647.02907273 kWh/year. Calendar normalization changes monthly timing.
Neither engine uses the installed AC count as quantified AC operating energy
in this fixture: it supplies no input power/hours. No future load is supplied.

The legacy engine has no hourly weather. Mixed states that apply hourly
self-use to legacy production distribute legacy monthly totals over v3's
synthetic solar-hour shape. This is an explicit diagnostic convention, not
an evidenced historical hourly profile. Both endpoints match their actual
engine; intermediate hybrids are diagnostic states, not purchasable packages
or unique causal explanations. Inverter AC is held at 5 kW for the bridge;
neither endpoint's synthetic output clips, so it adds no endpoint difference.
Actual package selection changes from a heuristic target to finite package
comparison maximizing undiscounted lifetime net. Size and price switches
describe the resulting selected systems, not a rerun of either selection rule.

What changed inside generation
At final 5.005 kWp, legacy production would be 6561.555 kWh. Removing its .95
unspecified-roof factor adds 345.345; changing yield from 1380 to 1398.23 adds
91.24115, for 6998.14115. Size alone at the legacy effective yield adds
662.055 kWh. A different order assigns the size/yield cross-effect differently.
The generation switch also changes monthly shares, so the financial row is
not merely annual extra kWh multiplied by one tariff. Important fixture detail:
roofDirection/roofSlope are absent even though explicit panel tilt15/azimuth180
are present. Legacy ignores those newer panel fields and uses unsure/unsure=.95;
v3 uses the explicit reference plane. Thus not all uplift is a revised solar
resource number, and it is inaccurate to call it an hourly-method correction.

Tariff result and excluded scenarios
For this October2026 ordinary1.2 fixture, old and new numeric schedules are
identical: tiers 200@3, next200@4.1584, rest@4.3583; Ft .1623; service24.62;
VAT7%. Tariff validation and provenance improved, but contribute THB0 here.
Do not generalize that zero to historical dates, different categories or TOU.
Personal tax/export/finance calculations live in separate optional scenarios.
The base engines supply no tax cash flow, no export receipts, and no repayment;
the exact net identity above independently checks their absence from this case.
Discount rate is absent/default0, and the reported net is undiscounted.

Evidence and limitations
Read: VERIFICATION_RESULTS.txt, REPRESENTATIVE_COMPARISONS.txt, ENERGY_SOURCES.txt,
TARIFF_SOURCES.txt and SCENARIO_SOURCES.txt; old/new estimator, energy engine,
tariff configurations, solar assumptions and resource snapshot configuration.
These are stored project evidence and implementation inspection, not a fresh
verification of changing external offers, tariff law or tax eligibility.

The dominant 97.67% self-use is plausible under the constructed smooth profiles,
but no meter/weather measurements validate it. Repeating a monthly mean solar
day and smooth generic demand suppresses variability; hourly averaging and
unmodeled clouds/occupancy can overstate coincidence. The size and direction of
that real-household bias cannot be quantified from this fixture alone.
The authored daytime55% share, intra-day shapes, 70/30 solar beam/diffuse mix,
fixed solar-time to civil-time alignment and representative2027 calendar lack
household calibration. JRC supports a reference annual/monthly modeled output,
not those household-hour coincidence assumptions. Geographic/mounting transfer
and shade factors remain planning assumptions. Other province/address inputs
do not personalize irradiance.

The single supplier's starting reference plus7% contingency is not a verified
complete VAT-inclusive site quotation; site extras and equipment compatibility
remain unconfirmed. Maintenance1.02% and degradation0.5% are transferred fixed
planning assumptions, not this installation's demonstrated costs/performance.
Constant tariffs over25years, undiscounted selection, absence of curtailment/
outages/availability losses and no measured calibration limit interpretation.

Defect disposition
No arithmetic defect is evidenced by this jump: both endpoints and all25 modern
gross-savings years reconcile to the existing engines within floating precision.
This investigation therefore changes no model math. The supported finding is
assumption sensitivity, dominated by self-use coincidence; altering it requires
an explicit calibrated/sensitivity design decision and evidence, not tuning the
model until the new lifetime number resembles the old one.
