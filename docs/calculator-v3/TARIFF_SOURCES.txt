Model boundaries

VAT remains the existing 7% modeling assumption. This is a monthly standard
charge estimate, not a billing replica: welfare credits, free-electricity
eligibility, arrears, penalties, and utility line-item rounding are excluded.
No TOU calculation: voltage, interval loads, utility holiday/substitute-holiday
calendar and full exception rules would be required. Explicit TOU or unknown
category returns unsupported. An omitted legacy category assumes ordinary
1.2 with a recorded warning; it does not infer category from bill amount.

Historical inversion resolves by the supplied bill calendar date. A missing bill
date uses the captured calculation-reference date as an explicit assumption, not
as a claim about the actual historical tariff. New assessments capture today's
Bangkok date; saved estimates retain their original explicit reference date.
Future savings uses that separate reference date; a constant tariff is a scenario,
not a price forecast. The low-level legacy resolver's omitted-date reference is
still frozen, but new calculation flows always supply today's or the saved date.
No date outside the supported period silently chooses a nearest tariff.
Invalid calendar dates are rejected. Dates are bill-month references, not
proration of a meter-reading interval. An old snapshot can describe its valid
historical bill after expiry; expiry prohibits extrapolating it to new dates.
