SolarMatch performance review - 2026-11-05
Scope: homeowner/installer presentation motion and public assets only.

Finding and fix: installer example feed queued requestAnimationFrame continuously
while paused, hovered, outside the viewport, and (when browser permitted) hidden.
New visibility-aware loop cancels pending frames while paused/hovered/offscreen/
hidden and restarts with zero elapsed time. IntersectionObserver and listeners
are disconnected on unmount. Animation appearance, 26px/s vertical / 32px/s
horizontal speed, repeated cards, user hold and reduced-motion controls remain.
Before: one feed callback per rendered frame in idle states (~60/s on 60Hz).
After: zero queued callbacks in those states (deterministic scheduler test),
with the normal per-frame callback while visible and playing. This is scheduling
evidence, not a measured browser FPS or battery improvement.

Home example count-up had no unmount frame cleanup (unlike the lifetime chart).
Added cancellation and run-token invalidation to prevent post-unmount updates.

Inspection: shared sun path coalesces scroll events into one frame and removes
listeners/ResizeObserver. Reveal hook disconnects IntersectionObserver. No
listener leak found there. Largest public asset inspected: terrace-house-1920.jpg
489,509 bytes; other leading files og.png 434,006 and home-hero.jpg 377,612.
No image recompression, animation quality reduction, dependency update or bundle
rewrite was justified by this review. Existing responsive hero is high priority.
