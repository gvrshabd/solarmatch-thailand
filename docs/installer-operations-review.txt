INSTALLER OPERATIONS REVIEW — LOCAL PREPARATION, NOT ACTIVATION

The deployment example remains fictitious and inactive. Production wrangler
configuration is unchanged. Do not enable its commented cron, live flags,
collection, payment, notification or delivery gates from this document.

Prepared implementation
- Scheduled invocation obtains a ten-minute wall-clock ownership lease before
  recording a run or doing allocation/reconciliation work. A valid overlapping
  invocation returns already_running and spends no retry attempt or credit.
- Ownership renews between bounded stages. Expired ownership cannot be renewed;
  an old owner cannot delete a replacement lease. Allocation retains existing
  per-enquiry leases and transactional distinct-recipient/ledger constraints.
- Notification dispatch rechecks ownership in the same final snapshot as the
  outbox lease, consent, classification, company link, settings and local clock.
  There is no async gap after that snapshot before the provider invocation.
  Ownership loss before dispatch releases the attempt unchanged. An already
  in-flight accepted provider request cannot be recalled; its evidence is retained.
- Payment reconciliation remains bounded provider reads with ledger idempotency;
  it never creates a charge or automatically repeats ambiguous authorization.

Protected admin review
- Last successful scheduled run is separate from successful manual allocation or
  notification work. INSTALLER_SCHEDULER_EXPECTED must remain false until an actual
  scheduler is deliberately approved and deployed. Missing runs are diagnostic
  after ten minutes; intentionally paused and closed-hour periods do not produce
  missed-run or stale-queue alerts. These thresholds are not retention policies.
  A latest failed scheduled invocation now reports failed rather than inheriting
  healthy from an earlier success; it requires attention and participates in the
  existing deduplicated scheduler alert. Paused/closed-hour priority is retained.
- Due queue older than thirty minutes, allocation failures, notification action
  required, ambiguous SMS, accepted-after-cancellation evidence and payment
  failure/manual association are shown separately. Age does not prove send or sale.
- The explicit capture control records deduplicated alerts containing safe codes and
  numerical counts only. No alert sender, webhook or external endpoint exists.
  Acknowledge records review, not resolution; persistent problems stay visible.
  Clearing a condition resolves its alert; recurrence creates a new record.
- Read-only launch preflight returns variable names and review categories, never
  values or secrets. Configuration presence is not verification or launch approval.
  Owner/legal/provider/deployment review remains explicitly blocked.

Before any separately authorised launch
Confirm installer origin, auth and cookie design including INSTALLER_BASE_URL;
review cron delivery, missed-trigger recovery and local-only alert-routing limits;
rehearse failures and provider acceptance ambiguity in approved sandbox; review
credentials and settlement, Thai SMS delivery/consent, LINE Official Account and
webhook configuration, company facts, tax, retention, purchased-credit/restoration
and cash-refund policies. Review private backup/restore and access controls. A
separate approval/design is required for external alert routing; local capture is
not a claim that anybody has been notified.

No production cron, provider request, refund, deployment or remote mutation was
authorised or performed by this preparation.
